Who we are

More than a fellowship. We are family.

NCCF Akwa Ibom State brings together Christian corps members who desire to know God deeply, serve faithfully, and make a meaningful difference during their service year.

Spiritually grounded

We grow through God's Word, prayer, worship, and discipleship.

Ready to serve

We use our gifts and time to bless people and strengthen communities.

Connected in love

We create friendships and an environment where everyone can belong.
